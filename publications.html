A Minimal Audit Log Schema for Tool-Using AI Agents

Bobie Brownie · 2026 · bobiebrownie3@gmail.com

Abstract

Tool-using AI agents produce records across planners, specialized workers, and external services. This paper proposes a compact audit log schema that links an agent action to its initiating request, tool invocation, and resulting artifact. The schema is intended as a conceptual basis for reconstructing complex workflows without retaining full private inputs.
